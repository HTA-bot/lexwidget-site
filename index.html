questions

Good to know.

Is there a subscription?

No. LexWidget is free for up to 30 words and one deck. Everything else is a single one-time purchase of $4.99 — no subscription, ever. You buy it once and keep it.

How does it sync across my devices?

Through your own iCloud. Your words move between your iPhone and Apple Watch automatically — quietly, and without any LexWidget account or server in the middle.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Yes. Your current word appears on the watch face and in the Smart Stack, with complications in every shape. The full Watch app is part of the Pro unlock.

What data do you collect?

None. There’s no account, no analytics, and no trackers. Auto-fill runs from a bundled dictionary on your device — nothing you add ever leaves it except through your own iCloud. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Which devices does it need?

iPhone on iOS 17 or later, and Apple Watch on watchOS 10 or later.
